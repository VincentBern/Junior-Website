Step 1 · The diagnostic

Where does your organization really stand?

Pick the diagnostic that matches your question. You answer on your own, at your own pace — your answers are saved on this device as you go — and you get a structured report as soon as you're done.

There are no right or wrong answers: an honest self-assessment is what makes the diagnostic useful. Your answers are only used to produce your report. Note: the written analysis in the report is currently delivered in French; scores and charts are shown in English.
